Greetings!!!
 
<div style="text-align: justify"> 
I am Farzad Ahmed, a Ph.D. student in Information Technology at <a href="https://www.gmu.edu/">George Mason University</a>, Fairfax, VA, USA, in the <a href="https://ist.gmu.edu/">Department of Information Sciences and Technology</a>. I am working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the MasonNLP research group under the supervision of <a href="https://volgenau.gmu.edu/profiles/ouzuner">Professor Özlem Uzuner</a>. My research focuses on agentic AI and multi-agent large language model systems, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM evaluation, applied to natural language processing and health informatics.<br /><br /></div>
 
**For additional information, please see the [CV](https://Farzad-1996.github.io/cv/) and [publications](https://Farzad-1996.github.io/publications/).**
