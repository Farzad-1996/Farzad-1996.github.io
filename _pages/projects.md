## Ongoing Research Projects
----------------
 
### Ph.D. Research, MasonNLP Research Group, George Mason University [2024 - *Current*]
-----------
* **Agentic AI for Medical Error Detection and Correction.** Five specialist reasoning agents orchestrated across a LangGraph DAG with sequential context dependencies, adjudicated by a coordinator constrained to cite only agent-flagged evidence, and grounded by retrieval-augmented generation over a FAISS vector index built from FDA drug labels ingested through the OpenFDA and RxNorm APIs. (*Python, LangGraph, LangChain, FAISS, LLM APIs, Pydantic*) &mdash; [Code](https://github.com/Farzad-1996/MedicalError)
* **LLM-Driven ETL to the OMOP Common Data Model.** Converting heterogeneous electronic health record databases into a standardized common data model, benchmarking seven large language models across MIMIC-IV, OpenMRS, and Synthea on executability, content fidelity, and referential integrity. (*Python, SQL, LLM APIs, OHDSI Athena*)
* **Cascade Prompting for Automated Document Classification.** A cascade prompting framework for classifying documents by type and content, with a comparative evaluation of open-weight and proprietary large language models. (*Python, LLM APIs, prompt engineering*) &mdash; [Code](https://github.com/Farzad-1996/healthequity)
* **Automated Identification of Incidentalomas from Radiology Reports.** Multi-anatomy evaluation of generative LLM-based and supervised approaches against a human inter-annotator agreement ceiling. (*Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, LoRA*)
* **Benchmarking and Evaluation of Large Language Models.** Controlled ablation design, paired significance testing, and confidence interval estimation for open-weight versus proprietary models under zero-shot, few-shot, and context-aware prompting. (*Python, PyTorch, HPC GPU clusters*)

## Earlier Research Projects
----------------
 
### Explainable AI, Computer Vision, and NLP [2019 - 2023]
-----------
* **Explainable Cost-Sensitive Deep Neural Networks for Brain Tumor Detection.** Cost-sensitive learning under severe class imbalance in brain MRI, with Grad-CAM interpretability. Published in *Multimedia Tools and Applications* (Springer), 2025. (*TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, Grad-CAM*)
* **Bengali License Plate Recognition.** License plate restoration and recognition combining a convolutional network with GFP-GAN image enhancement. Published at ICCIT 2023. (*Python, PyTorch, OpenCV*)
* **Interpretable Movie Review Analysis.** Sentiment classification with machine learning and transformer models, explained with LIME and SHAP. Published at IEEE CSDE 2022. (*Python, Hugging Face Transformers, Scikit-learn, LIME, SHAP*)
* **Bengali News Headline Categorization.** Empirical comparison of machine learning techniques for multi-class categorization of Bengali news headlines. Published at ICCIT 2022. (*Python, Scikit-learn, NLTK*)
* **An Empirical Study on How Developers Discussed Pandas Topics.** Topic modeling and analysis of developer discussion text. Published at MIET 2022. (*Python, LDA topic modeling, Scikit-learn*)
* **Interpretable Retinal Disease Classification from OCT Images.** Deep neural network classification of retinal disease with explainable AI. Published at ICECIT 2021. (*Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Grad-CAM*)
* **A Comprehensive Survey of COVID-19 Detection Using Medical Images.** Survey of deep learning approaches to COVID-19 detection from X-ray and CT imaging. Published in *SN Computer Science* (Springer), 2021.
* **Early Depression Detection from Social Media.** B.Sc. thesis project applying deep learning to detect early signs of depression in user-generated text. Published at IEEE TENSYMP 2020. (*Python, TensorFlow, Keras*) &mdash; [Thesis](https://Farzad-1996.github.io/files/Farzad_B.Sc._Thesis.pdf)
